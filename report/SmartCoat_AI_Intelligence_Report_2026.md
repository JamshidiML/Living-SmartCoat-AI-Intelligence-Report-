# Executive Brief

This independent repository contains public-source strategic intelligence for SmartCoat. It remains separate from `smartcoat-intelligence` and contains no confidential formulations, customer data, credentials or application code.

## Current strategic thesis

SmartCoat should become a traceable industrial decision system connecting materials, formulation, process, inspection, experiments, suppliers and human decisions. Durable value comes from governed data, ontology, validated workflows and measurable factory outcomes.

## Weekly update — 6–13 September 2026

The week confirms five priorities: operational AI governance; resilient sovereign infrastructure; deployment-led industrial AI; supplier and compute resilience; and practical textile inspection.

- EU–U.S. regulatory divergence makes jurisdiction, provenance, human oversight and evidence packs commercially material.
- ENISA evaluation of advanced models reinforces adversarial testing and permission boundaries for industrial copilots.
- UAE AI infrastructure planning is moving toward distributed and hardened architectures; factory AI should include local fallback and safe degraded modes.
- Germany–UAE industrial ties strengthen the value of a reusable German pilot-to-Gulf evidence package.
- Saudi Humain's reported financing and IPO preparation strengthen the sovereign-AI scale-up signal.
- Textile-defect research continues to reward controlled imaging, compact models, region-aware features and disciplined evaluation.

## Priority actions

1. Freeze one textile benchmark with closed-set, anomaly, open-world and zero-shot tracks.
2. Draft the first Compliance Evidence Pack and agent red-team checklist.
3. Add offline/degraded-mode acceptance tests to factory AI pilots.
4. Extend the supplier ontology with disruption scenarios and alternate-source fields.
5. Package German pilot evidence for later UAE/Saudi partner discussions.
6. Add jurisdiction, action authority and evidence provenance to every AI event.
