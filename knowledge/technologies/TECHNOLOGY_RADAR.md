## Priority shifts — 2 August 2026

### English

1. **Portability becomes a formal pilot acceptance criterion.** EU gigafactory procurement and U.S. semiconductor incentives point toward multiple clouds, processors and runtimes rather than one stable dominant deployment stack.
2. **Experiment lineage moves upward.** U.S. investment in integrated data systems for AI-driven science reinforces that reusable, linked evidence must precede autonomous materials recommendations.
3. **Open-world inspection remains experimental, not production-ready.** OW-DLN is directly relevant to unknown textile defects, but line-scan speed, coated surfaces and imaging drift still require independent validation.
4. **Compliance remains urgent after simplification.** The EU AI Omnibus creates more room for supervised testing while preserving the need for intended-use records, human oversight and change control.
