## Priority shifts — 9 August 2026

### English

1. **Compliance becomes operational architecture.** AI system cards, decision boundaries, model/data versions, human override and change logs should be generated as part of deployment.
2. **Portability remains a formal acceptance criterion.** The first inspection pipeline should pass the same frozen benchmark on two runtime or accelerator paths.
3. **Factory integration becomes a moat metric.** Measure how many process variables, operator actions, QC decisions and downstream test results are linked to each AI event.
4. **Closed-loop scientific AI moves upward.** The strategic signal is strong, but SmartCoat should prepare experiment contracts and lineage rather than attempt premature autonomous formulation.
5. **Open-world inspection remains experimental.** Continue shadow-mode validation before production authority.
