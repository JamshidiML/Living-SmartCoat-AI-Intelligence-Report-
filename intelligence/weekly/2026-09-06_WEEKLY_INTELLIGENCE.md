# Weekly Intelligence Review — 6 September 2026

## Executive signal
Europe is expanding sovereign AI compute while industrial AI funding continues to concentrate in physical deployment, edge inference and infrastructure. The strongest SmartCoat implication is to keep the product layer portable and evidence-driven: factory value will come from traceable context, inspection workflows and domain data, not from owning a specific model or accelerator.
